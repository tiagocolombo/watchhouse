# ADR 0018: Recovery and follow-through independent of repair execution

Accepted 2026-11-15. Builds on PR #12's durable fake protocol. Qualified real worker/runtime/credential access remains unavailable; complete the independent M5 records rather than bypass M4 admission.

Use explicit commander-authored prospective recovery criteria and bounded observation windows. Evaluate numeric reported traffic, errors, latency and coverage; insufficient traffic/coverage is inconclusive. An independently attributed reviewer supplies their own distinct new evidence before commander resolution. Bind the plan to the source digest, incident revision and case policy; changed context/challenges invalidate acceptance and reopen resolved response for reassessment. Mark this as operator-reported review, not product-observed telemetry.

Keep corrective actions alive after response resolution. Require acceptance criteria, owner, distinct assigned verifier, deadline and independent reported proof. Preserve waivers/residual risk distinctly from verified completion. Deadline escalation works without models and is retained even when late proof arrives. Scope deployment reports explicitly; never infer deployment or recovery from merge.

Mitigation remains a structured blocked proposal with prerequisites, target, risk, stop/rollback and owned escalation. A record cannot grant production execution authority. Append-only recovery records, atomic current state/events/receipts, CLI/API/web visibility and escaped Markdown support real operator workflows and deterministic demos. Actual mitigation/deployment integrations and authenticated telemetry collection remain separate unimplemented boundaries; postmortem/knowledge acceptance follows this slice.
