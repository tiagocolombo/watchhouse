# ADR 0038: Restore facts without inheriting deployment authority

Status: accepted implementation choice, 2026-11-21. Extends ADR 0037's staged backup work.

Restore a verified, independently hash-pinned v1 backup into a new private directory outside its input. Reverify the bounded copied payload before opening it. A persistent incomplete marker prevents coordinator startup throughout copy, supported migration and isolation; completed exports also reject normal coordinator startup in their reserved snapshot layout. Preserve the original manifest as provenance. Retain failed destinations without overwrite or automatic deletion.

Migration 032 adds append-only restore audit, permanent configuration/resource fences, immutable artifact location overlays, explicit bounded publisher read permits and durable notification pacing. Known schema-31 backups can migrate forward to the current known history. Immutable source/candidate record bytes and their identities are retained; verified materializations relocate through overlays, including repeated restore. No original worker control directory, runtime or auth home is copied, read for authority, revoked or adopted during restore.

All copied source, notification, model, container, publisher, merger, operations, repair-source and worker configuration revisions remain fenced after a normal pause is resumed. Native admission is cleared. Fresh OS-installed revisions and new physical qualification are required; reinstalling an old revision does not clear its fence. Old native handles, including terminal handles, cannot enter automatic observation/cleanup. Nonterminal resources remain unknown/quarantined and consume pinned snapshot-scope capacity until new, post-restore operator-reported inspection closes them. Such a report is not native cleanup proof or execution admission.

Expire old fake proof/lease progress without extending deadlines. Retain canonical transactional records, command receipts, budgets and external identities; recompute evidence byte counters and source currency, relocate files, and let the deterministic supervisor process owned overdue duties. Stream large policy/job/escalation histories rather than collecting them in memory.

A snapshot can precede effects by the original coordinator. Pending publication, merge and runbook records become uncertain with fresh local fences and owned review escalation. Explicit reconciliation observes stable original identities without replay. Publisher credentials are not used automatically: an operator grants a five-minute read-only permit; existing stages can then be inspected through completion without gaining write authority. Reconciliation records the current backend's provenance, so injected observations cannot inherit a stale native flag. Observed stale success never establishes deployment or service recovery.

Copied notification routes are held, inflight sends become uncertain, and old unrouted escalations do not automatically attach to a new destination. Fresh reviewed reminders use a distinct durable source link/receipt and preserve old obligations and delivery uncertainty. All new sends after restore share at least a 30-second durable reservation interval, including across process restarts. Installing fresh credentials alone cannot resend an old alert backlog.

The original coordinator/worker deployment must be retired or independently reconciled by its operator before new live authority is activated. Restore cannot infer its remote state or silently terminate it. No service credentials, protected-repository authority, live gateway or actual account is activated by these changes. Fixtures and explicitly delegated native CI drills remain separate evidence classes.

Validation also exposed a live-clock race: container selection sampled time before asynchronous cleanup/SQLite contention. Read time after obtaining the write reservation; health samples time after reading its retained watermark. This preserves actual backward-clock denial while avoiding stale-sample false holds. The regression advances the clock through cleanup and requires admission once it has caught up; it does not waive the gate.
