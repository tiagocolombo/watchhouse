# Incident record

Artifact metadata: ID/version, incident/case IDs, owner, reviewer, timestamps, sensitivity, source snapshot.

## Current brief

- State and severity/profile, with rationale and uncertainty.
- Affected services, environments, releases, dependencies, and critical workflows.
- Customer/business impact; measured values and estimation method.
- Incident commander, operations lead, communications lead, scribe.
- Current objectives, actions, blockers, next update time.
- Investigation, change, escalation, and source links.

## Impact and timing

Record known/estimated start, detection, acknowledgment, declaration, mitigation, and recovery times with their source evidence. Keep unknown timestamps unknown.

Record affected users/requests/accounts or other appropriate measures, time window, denominator, data freshness, and coverage limits. Separate measured impact from inferred risk.

## Response plan and communication

Objectives and assigned owners; current hypotheses; mitigation options; authorized actions; stop conditions; escalation chain; next operational and stakeholder updates.

For each update, retain audience, draft/version, approval if required, destination, sent time, and actual delivery status.

## Recovery assessment

Criteria established; indicators and critical workflows checked; traffic sufficiency; deployment/release mapping; observation window; remaining impact/risk; verified/partial/ineffective/inconclusive result.

Commander decision, evidence, exceptions/risk acceptance, reopening triggers, and handoff to monitoring.

## Continuing work

Postmortem applicability and deadline; linked problem/known error; permanent repairs; corrective actions with accountable owners; unresolved questions. Incident resolution does not close these automatically.
