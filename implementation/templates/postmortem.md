# Post-incident review

Artifact metadata: ID/version, incident, postmortem owner, independent reviewers, due date, status, sensitivity, evidence snapshot.

## Executive summary

What happened, actual impact, duration/uncertainty, recovery, and continuing risk. Link the incident record. Keep causal claims proportional to evidence.

## Impact and response chronology

Quantified impact with denominator, time window, method, and limitations. Timeline of contributing conditions, detection, acknowledgment, declaration, major actions, communications, mitigation, and recovery. Every material entry links to evidence.

## Contributing factors and causal analysis

Explain mechanisms and conditions, including dependencies, design, configuration, capacity, testing, instrumentation, and process where supported.

Distinguish established causes, plausible factors, disproven theories, and unresolved questions. Analyze systemic conditions without assigning personal blame. Do not use an agent's agreement as causal evidence.

## Response assessment

What helped; detection gaps; delayed decisions; unsuccessful actions; communication/handoff/escalation performance; authorization or tooling limitations; why mitigation worked or failed.

## Knowledge and review

Known-error/runbook/lesson proposals; applicability and source evidence; accepted knowledge changes; unresolved objections; reviewer dispositions; postmortem acceptance decision.

## Follow-through

Schedule effectiveness review and recurrence checks. Report overdue actions after incident resolution. Record whether implemented improvements changed detection, impact, or recovery.
