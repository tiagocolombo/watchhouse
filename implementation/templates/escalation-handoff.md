# Escalation and handoff record

Artifact metadata: ID/version, incident/assignment/run, accountable owner, timestamps, sensitivity, snapshot.

## Trigger

Block reason code; detected by agent/supervisor; last heartbeat; last useful progress; deadline; impact of delay; uncertainty about external actions.

## Work already performed

Objective; evidence; hypotheses tested; failed approaches; open challenges; current patch and base/head references; workspace/checkpoint; runtime/profile/version; consumed/remaining limits.

## Requested response

Assistance or decision required; functional/coordination/severity/authority/dependency/human path; urgency; recipient; acknowledgment deadline; response deadline; fallback chain.

## Notification record

| Attempt | Destination | Sent at | Delivery state | Acknowledged by/at | Next action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pending | Pending | Pending | Pending | Pending | Pending |

A delivery receipt is not acceptance; acknowledgment is not resolution.

## Transfer controls

Previous executor stop/quarantine confirmation; credentials revoked; uncertain external effects reconciled; mutable resources isolated; previous/new lease and fence references.

New owner, acceptance time, received checkpoint, remaining obligations and budget. Until acceptance, the previous accountable response owner remains responsible.

## Resolution

Action taken; authority and rationale; outcome/evidence; remaining blocker; next review/escalation time. Reassignment cannot silently reset incident deadlines.
