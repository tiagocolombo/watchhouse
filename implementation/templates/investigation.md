# Investigation record

Artifact metadata: ID/version, incident/case, investigator, independent challenger, timestamps, sensitivity, snapshot.

## Question and context

Observed symptoms; affected scope; recent changes; reliable facts; unknowns; relevant accepted knowledge and its versions.

## Hypothesis register

| ID | Claim | Supporting evidence | Counterevidence | State | Next discriminating experiment | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pending | Pending | Pending | Pending | Proposed | Pending | Pending |

States include proposed, testing, supported, refuted, and inconclusive. Supported does not mean proven universally.

## Experiment

For each experiment record:

- Question and hypothesis references.
- Preconditions, target scope, authorization, risk and resource limits.
- Expected results under competing explanations.
- Exact method, command/query, environment and artifact versions.
- Actual start/end, observed result, evidence digest, limitations.
- Interpretation, contradictions, and next action.

Preserve failed attempts so a new responder does not repeat them unknowingly.

## Challenges and decisions

Contested claims; challenger and assignee; evidence requested; due date; answer; independent disposition; remaining dissent. Link resulting decision records.

## Investigation conclusion

What is established, what is likely, what was ruled out, and what remains unknown. Recommend mitigation/repair/monitoring/escalation with evidence and authority requirements. Do not force a single root cause.
